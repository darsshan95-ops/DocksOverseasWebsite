How we ship

The cold chain,
step by step.

Fresh produce forgives nothing. Every stage below exists to protect the few days of shelf life between a farm gate and your shelf.

01

Sourcing at the farm gate

We buy from growers we return to season after season, and we walk the plots before the harvest window opens. Price is agreed against a grade, not a guess.

Grading and packing

Sorted to your count, calibre and colour spec. Cartons, liners and labels made up to your market's rules.

Pre-cooling and cold store

Field heat comes out before anything moves. Each line holds its own pull-down temperature, and reefer set points are recorded on the booking, not left to the terminal.

Documents and clearance

Invoice and packing list prepared ahead of the cut-off. We clear at origin ourselves, so the box does not sit waiting on paper.

Sailing and handover

Reefer or air, whichever the shelf life and the price point justify. You get the booking, the container number and a straight answer if anything slips.

Where we go

Rooted in India,
pointed outward.

We buy in the growing belts, consolidate at our Indian load ports, and sail for the Gulf, Europe and East Africa — mixing lines into one container where the volumes suit, without the produce ever leaving temperature.
