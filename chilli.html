Green and red chilli exporter from India

Green and red chilli picked to your heat and length specification, pre-cooled the same day it leaves the field, and moved by air or reefer depending on the shelf life you need.

Vegetable · Exported from India

Grading and packing

Long greenByadgiBulletJwalaSame-day pre-cool

Chilli is the line that punishes slow handling hardest. Ours goes from field to pre-cooler the same day, sorted for length, colour and freedom from anthracnose, then packed in ventilated cartons with the liner chosen for the transit time rather than whatever is on the shelf.

Specification

Green & red chilli at a glance

SeasonYear round, with regional shifts through the year
VarietiesLong green, bullet, Byadgi red, Jwala
Packing5 kg / 10 kg ventilated corrugated carton with perforated liner
Temperature8 – 10 °C at 90 – 95% relative humidity
Shelf life2 – 3 weeks held at temperature
LoadabilityApprox. 14 – 16 MT per 40 ft reefer
ModeAir freight for short windows; 40 ft reefer where shelf life allows

How it travels

Below about 7 °C chilli takes chilling injury and pits; above 12 °C it loses turgor fast. That narrow band is why we fix the set point on the booking and why short-window orders go by air even though the freight costs more — a cheap reefer that lands limp fruit is not cheap.

Where we ship it

Green & red chilli for the Gulf, Europe and beyond

We load for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain, and into the UK, the Netherlands and Germany. Smaller buyers can take part of a mixed container alongside our other lines rather than committing to a full load of one item.
