Pomegranate exporter from India

Deep-arilled Bhagwa and Ganesh pomegranate, colour-graded and counted to the box, shipped from India under an unbroken cold chain.

Fruit · Exported from India

Grading and packing

BhagwaGaneshReeferAir freight

We grade on colour break and calibre rather than weight alone, because a box that looks uneven on opening costs you the repeat order. Fruit is counted to the box, packed with foam nets and corner protection so the crown survives handling, and lidded cartons are palletised to your market's pallet spec.

Specification

Pomegranate at a glance

VarietiesBhagwa, Ganesh
Sizes200 – 250 g (medium) · 250 – 300 g (large) · 300 – 350 g and above (extra large)
Packing3.5 kg / 4 kg corrugated carton, 9 – 18 count depending on grade
Temperature5 – 7 °C at 90 – 95% relative humidity
Shelf life8 – 12 weeks held at temperature
LoadabilityApprox. 4,800 boxes per 40 ft reefer
Mode40 ft reefer; air freight for samples and trial lots

How it travels

Field heat comes out before anything is palletised. Pomegranate is forgiving compared with soft fruit, but it bruises at the crown and splits if humidity swings, so the reefer set point and vent position go on the booking rather than being left to the terminal. Gulf transits typically run three to five days from an Indian west-coast port.

Where we ship it

Pomegranate for the Gulf, Europe and beyond

We load for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain, and into the UK, the Netherlands and Germany. Smaller buyers can take part of a mixed container alongside our other lines rather than committing to a full load of one item.
