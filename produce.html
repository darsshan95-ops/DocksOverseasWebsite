The lines we carry.

Everything below ships from our own sourcing. Seasons are indicative — growing windows shift with the weather, and we will tell you honestly when a line is off or when quality is not worth the freight.

01 — Fruit

Pomegranate

BhagwaGaneshReeferAir freight

Dark red arils, thin skin, and the sugar that makes the fruit sell itself. We grade on colour break and calibre, count to the box, and pack with foam nets and corner protection so the crown survives the trip.

02 — Vegetable

Green & red chilli

Long greenByadgiBulletSame-day pre-cool

A fast line that punishes slow handling. Ours goes from field to pre-cooler the same day, packed in ventilated cartons with liners chosen for the transit time — air for the short window, reefer where the shelf life allows.

03 — Fruit

Husked coconut

Semi-huskedFully huskedDry container

Selected on shell integrity and water content, trimmed clean and free of mould at the eyes. A forgiving line to ship and a steady one to sell — which is exactly why it is worth buying from someone who still checks.

04 — Fruit

Cavendish banana

G9 CavendishGreen shippedCluster packed

Cut at the calliper your market wants, de-handed, washed, and packed in cluster trays. Shipped green at 13 – 14 °C so the ripening decision stays yours, with vent settings agreed before the box is booked.

05 — Vegetable

Suran · elephant foot yam

WholeCuredLong shelf life

Cured after lifting so the skin sets and the tuber travels. A dependable South Asian retail line across the Gulf, and one of the few fresh items that gives you real breathing room on shelf life.
